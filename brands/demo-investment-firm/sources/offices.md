# Offices at Kasauti Capital Partners

Fictional, like the firm, and every figure is made up.

- Office A covers Asia Pacific. Its head is Office Head A.
- Office B covers Europe. Its head is Office Head B.
- Office C covers the Middle East and Africa. Its head is Office Head C.
- Office D covers North America. Its head is Office Head D.
- Office E covers Latin America. Its head is Office Head E.
- The Analyst Lead collects the five offices' figures and signs off every report.

`quarterly_aum.csv`, in this folder, has each office's assets under management at the end of each of its last five quarters, in USD millions, with one row for each office and quarter. Q1 FY27 is the latest quarter in it. It has no row for the quarter being collected now, because the offices are still sending it.
