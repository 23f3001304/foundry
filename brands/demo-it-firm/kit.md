Morpankh Systems is a fictional IT services firm, made up for this demo along with everything in this folder. It is not a real company. Morpankh is Hindi for peacock feather. Harsingar Stores, the client its scene names, is made up too.

Use the paper colour for backgrounds and the ink colour for text. Keep the peacock teal for headings and large shapes, and use the gold once on a page, for the one thing that matters most.

Put the logo on the paper colour. On the teal use `logo-on-dark.png`, the same logo reversed, and never `logo.png`. Leave clear space around it, at least a quarter of its width.

Name the team and the sprint on every status line. Write numbers as digits. Say "blocked on QA", or whatever reason the person gave, in their own words, and never say a person is late. An input nobody sent is written as "not received", with no figure in its place.
